Connecting Local Farmers with Conscious Consumers

Discover Local. Eat Fresh. MarketLink – eGreen Basket solves produce waste and eliminates exploitative middleman fees by enabling direct pre-orders for weekend farmers markets.

The Challenge

Why MarketLink Was Created

Every week, passionate organic growers travel from Potohar, Malir, Swat, and Chakwal to set up stalls at local weekend farmers markets. However, without predictability, farmers frequently face unsold perishable produce, while consumers arrive to find popular items already sold out.

MarketLink bridges this gap. Customers can preview the farmer's weekly stock, reserve their favorite produce online, and collect their customized basket during designated pickup windows — paying directly in cash at the stall.

Find Your Market Meet The Farmers
Zero Payment Gateways

We don't hold your money or take transaction cuts. You inspect your fresh produce in person and pay the farmer directly in cash upon collection.

Unmatched Freshness

Because farmers know their exact pre-orders in advance, produce is harvested right before market day — not stored for weeks in cold storage.

Fair Value for Growers

100% of the produce value stays with the farmer. We celebrate smallholders, women agri-entrepreneurs, and traditional food artisans.
